Support

For help with TouchTips, email rathiharivansh@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Remove private contact details from any screenshots.

Find your people

Allow Contacts access during setup. Choose the list or timeline in TouchTips settings, or open the map to browse by meeting place.

Correct a meeting

Open a person to change their meeting date, place, or note. Automatic suggestions can be incomplete: iOS does not notify a suspended app about every contact change. After adding a contact, return to TouchTips and review the details.

Change permissions

Manage Contacts, Location, and Notifications in iPhone Settings. Location helps with place suggestions. Notifications are optional.

Remove saved details

Swipe left on a person and confirm to remove their meeting details and note. To clear all TouchTips records, choose “Delete all data” in the app’s settings. Neither action deletes your iPhone contacts.
